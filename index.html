A few things you might be wondering

We’re a little camera shy. Is that okay?+

Absolutely. You can ease into it at your own pace. Gentle prompts and a little movement help a session feel natural, with plenty of space to just be together.

What should we wear?+

Start with something you feel good in. Soft colors, comfortable textures, and outfits that complement each other photograph beautifully. We can talk through ideas when planning your session.

Where will our session take place?+

Somewhere that feels like you. An open field, a favorite trail, a meaningful campus, or a place you love spending time together. Share your ideas in your inquiry and we’ll plan from there.

How do I find out about pricing and availability?+

Start with the inquiry below. Include your session type and any dates you’re considering so we can talk through availability, session details, and pricing together.
